5 · Stress test: does each controller cope with different meals?

A "precision" controller should handle a small and a large meal safely, not only the one it was tuned on. Overshoot = water pushed too far basic (like a dangerous low in diabetes). The overshoot limit (60) is an assumption until you run a real overdose test.
